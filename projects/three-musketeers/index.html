Three Musketeers

Rules

  • Musketeers (🤺, black) move first. A Musketeer's only legal move is capturing an orthogonally-adjacent Enemy.
  • Enemies (🪖, white) can only slide onto an orthogonally-adjacent empty square — they can never capture a Musketeer.
  • Enemies win if they force all 3 Musketeers into the same row or column.
  • Musketeers win if, on their turn, they have no legal move and are not aligned in a row or column.
